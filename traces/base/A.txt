FINAL ANSWER: Claude Shannon was born on April 30, 1916, and died on February 24, 2001.
Three key contributions to information theory:
1. He laid the theoretical foundations for digital circuits and information theory.
2. He introduced the term "bit" (binary digit) as a fundamental unit of information.
3. He authored the seminal paper "A Mathematical Theory of Communication" in 1948, which formalized
   the concept of information, introduced the idea of channel capacity, and established the
   noisy-channel coding theorem, enabling reliable digital communication over noisy channels.
