# Super 30 (2019)

## Overview
Super 30 is a 2019 Indian biographical drama film directed by Vikas Bahl and starring Hrithik Roshan as mathematician and educator Anand Kumar. The film depicts Kumar's Super 30 programme in Patna, Bihar, in which he trains 30 underprivileged students each year to crack the Indian Institute of Technology entrance examination (JEE). The film was a major commercial success.

## Plot
Anand Kumar (Hrithik Roshan) is a brilliant mathematics student from a poor family in Patna. When his father dies and he is unable to afford the trip to Cambridge to pursue his mathematical education, he begins teaching to support his family. He becomes involved with a corrupt coaching institute run by a powerful local businessman. After a moral reckoning, Anand breaks away and starts his own initiative — Super 30 — selecting 30 talented but economically disadvantaged students each year and teaching them for free, providing food and lodging.

The film follows Anand's struggle against the entrenched interests of the coaching industry and local political power as his programme achieves extraordinary results. The coaching mafia, threatened by his success, actively tries to sabotage and intimidate him. Anand's perseverance and his students' ultimate triumph at the IIT JEE become the film's climax.

## Themes
- **Education as liberation**: Access to quality education as the most powerful tool against poverty and caste inequality
- **Class conflict**: The fundamental conflict between those who benefit from educational inequality and those who challenge it
- **Idealism vs corruption**: Anand's incorruptible commitment against the venal coaching industry
- **Mathematics as beauty**: The film conveys genuine love for mathematics as an intellectual and democratic art
- **Rural aspirations**: The hunger of talented children from small towns for access to India's elite institutions

## Critical Reception
Super 30 was praised for Hrithik Roshan's committed performance, particularly his physical and vocal transformation. The film sparked discussion about India's educational inequality and the coaching industry's extractive economics.
