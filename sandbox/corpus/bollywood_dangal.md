# Dangal (2016)

## Overview
Dangal is a 2016 Indian biographical sports drama film directed by Nitesh Tiwari. The film stars Aamir Khan as Mahavir Singh Phogat, a former national-level wrestler who trains his daughters Geeta (Fatima Sana Shaikh) and Babita (Sanya Malhotra) to become world-class wrestlers. Dangal became the highest-grossing Indian film of all time, earning over Rs 2,000 crore worldwide.

## Plot
Set in the village of Balali in Haryana, the film follows Mahavir Phogat, who dreamed of winning a gold medal for India in wrestling but was forced to abandon the sport due to financial constraints. He transfers his ambition to his sons — but has only daughters. When he discovers that his daughters can fight, he makes the unconventional decision to train them as wrestlers despite fierce social opposition from villagers who mock the idea of girls wrestling.

The film traces Geeta's and Babita's gruelling training under their father's demanding regime — waking at dawn, cutting their hair short, wearing shorts in a conservative village. Geeta goes on to win Commonwealth gold. The film also portrays the complication of Geeta's relationship with her father when she goes to the national academy and begins to question his methods, before their reconciliation at the climactic Commonwealth Games final.

## Themes
- **Gender and sport**: The film is fundamentally about breaking gender barriers in a deeply patriarchal social context
- **Parental ambition vs child's autonomy**: The complex, sometimes painful dynamics of a parent who pushes children to achieve dreams they may not have chosen
- **National identity and pride**: Wrestling as a form of patriotic expression
- **Rural India and change**: The tension between traditional village conservatism and the wider world
- **Class and opportunity**: Sport as a path out of poverty for rural girls with few other options

## Critical Reception
Dangal was a phenomenon. Beyond its commercial success, it sparked national conversations about gender, sport, and girls' education. Aamir Khan's physical transformation and Fatima Sana Shaikh's athletic performance were widely praised.
