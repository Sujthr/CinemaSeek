# Joker (2019)

## Overview
Joker is a 2019 American psychological thriller film directed and co-written by Todd Phillips. The film stars Joaquin Phoenix as Arthur Fleck, a struggling stand-up comedian and clown-for-hire in a decaying Gotham City who descends into violence and becomes the eponymous supervillain. The film won the Golden Lion at the Venice Film Festival, the Academy Award for Best Actor (Joaquin Phoenix), and Best Original Score. It grossed over $1 billion worldwide.

## Plot
Arthur Fleck (Joaquin Phoenix) is a deeply damaged man in 1981 Gotham City — he lives with his delusional mother, suffers from a condition that causes uncontrollable laughing fits, and works as a clown while trying to become a stand-up comedian. He is kicked, beaten, humiliated, and finally fired. He has a fantasy relationship with his neighbour that proves entirely imaginary.

When Arthur kills three bullying Wall Street men on a subway, the act — unintentionally — becomes a catalyst for a class revolt simmering beneath Gotham's surface. The film traces Arthur's spiral from isolated, invisible victim to performative, empowered killer who becomes a symbol for the city's resentment of the wealthy.

The film's ending is deliberately ambiguous: it is unclear how much of what we witnessed was real and how much was fantasy inside Arthur's institutionalised mind.

## Themes
- **Class resentment and systemic failure**: Arthur is the product of a society that discards its mentally ill, its poor, and its vulnerable
- **Mental illness and isolation**: The film depicts untreated mental illness with uncomfortable intimacy
- **The creation of evil**: What conditions produce a person capable of mass violence?
- **Ambiguous reality**: The film's unreliable narrator structure means the audience can never be certain what happened
- **Class conflict and uprising**: The "clown uprising" in Gotham mirrors real-world class resentment
