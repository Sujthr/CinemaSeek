# URI: The Surgical Strike (2019)

## Overview
URI: The Surgical Strike is a 2019 Indian Hindi-language action film directed by Aditya Dhar and starring Vicky Kaushal as Major Vihaan Singh Shergill. The film is based on the 2016 surgical strikes carried out by the Indian Army in response to the Uri attack by Pakistani militants. URI became one of the highest-grossing Bollywood films of 2019.

## Plot
The film begins with the URI attack — Pakistani militants attack an Indian Army base in Uri, Kashmir, killing 19 soldiers. Major Vihaan Singh Shergill, grieving a personal loss while trying to care for his mother who has Alzheimer's, is assigned to plan and execute a covert retaliatory surgical strike across the Line of Control.

The film unfolds as a procedural military thriller, detailing the intelligence gathering, planning, and execution of the cross-border operation. Vihaan assembles a team of special forces soldiers and navigates political, logistical, and human challenges to deliver the strike. The film climaxes with the actual surgical strikes — conducted with precision — and their aftermath.

## Themes
- **Military honour and national service**: The film celebrates the courage and sacrifice of India's armed forces
- **Personal loss and duty**: Vihaan's private grief coexists with his professional commitment
- **Modern warfare**: The shift from conventional military operations to intelligence-led, covert strikes
- **Institutional trust**: The film portrays civilian and military leadership as aligned in purpose
- **Grief and dementia**: A subplot involving Vihaan's mother provides human texture to the military narrative

## Critical Reception
URI: The Surgical Strike was a massive commercial success and received favourable reviews for Vicky Kaushal's performance and the film's pacey direction. The film's catchphrase "How's the josh?" became a cultural phenomenon.
