# Spotlight (2015)

## Overview
Spotlight is a 2015 American drama film directed by Tom McCarthy. The film stars Michael Keaton, Mark Ruffalo, Rachel McAdams, Live Schreiber, John Slattery, and Stanley Tucci. Based on the true story of The Boston Globe's "Spotlight" investigative journalism team that exposed the Catholic Church's systematic cover-up of child sexual abuse by priests, the film won the Academy Award for Best Picture and Best Original Screenplay.

## Plot
In 2001, Marty Baron (Liev Schreiber), the new editor of The Boston Globe, assigns the Spotlight team — led by Walter Robinson (Michael Keaton) and including reporters Michael Rezendes (Mark Ruffalo), Sacha Pfeiffer (Rachel McAdams), and Matt Carroll (Brian d'Arcy James) — to investigate a single case: a priest accused of abuse. What begins as a single case opens into a systemic investigation.

The reporters discover not just isolated abusers but a vast institutional cover-up: the Archdiocese of Boston knew of abuse by dozens of priests and systematically moved them to new parishes rather than reporting them to police. Church lawyers negotiated secret settlements with victims, binding them to silence. Local institutions — lawyers, judges, newspapers — were implicated in a culture of deference to Church authority.

The film builds with the restrained urgency of journalism: document by document, source by source, the scale of the cover-up becomes undeniable.

## Themes
- **Institutional power and complicity**: How respected institutions — the Church, the legal system, the press itself — can protect abusers through deference and silence
- **Class and community**: Boston's Catholic community as a tight network where challenging the Church meant challenging one's own social fabric
- **Journalism as public good**: The film is a tribute to slow, careful, unglamorous investigative reporting
- **Complicity and conscience**: The reporters grapple with their own earlier failures to pursue the story
- **Ensemble commitment**: The film functions as an ensemble drama — no single hero, a team
