# Kapoor and Sons (2016)

## Overview
Kapoor and Sons (Since 1921) is a 2016 Indian Hindi-language family drama film directed by Shakun Batra and produced by Karan Johar under Dharma Productions. The film stars Rishi Kapoor, Rajat Kapoor, Ratna Pathak Shah, Sidharth Malhotra, Fawad Khan, and Alia Bhatt. It is a multigenerational ensemble drama about a dysfunctional family forced together by crisis.

## Plot
The Kapoor family is brought together at their old family home in Coonoor when their 90-year-old patriarch Dadu (Rishi Kapoor) has a near-fatal heart attack. The two brothers, Rahul (Fawad Khan), a successful novelist living abroad, and Arjun (Sidharth Malhotra), a struggling writer in London, return home after years apart. Their parents, Sunita (Ratna Pathak Shah) and Harsh (Rajat Kapoor), have a marriage in quiet collapse.

What begins as a family reunion becomes an excavation of old wounds: parental favouritism, buried resentments, unexpressed desires, and secrets each family member has carried in silence. Tia (Alia Bhatt), a local girl who befriends both brothers, becomes a lens through which the family's fractures are refracted. The film builds toward a charged confrontation where everything suppressed finally surfaces.

## Themes
- **Family as site of love and damage**: The film presents family not as sanctuary but as the place where we are most vulnerable and most hurt
- **Secrets and identity**: Each character carries something unspoken that shapes their every interaction
- **Sibling rivalry and parental preference**: The invisible wounds of not being the favoured child
- **Marriage in crisis**: The film honestly depicts a long marriage drained of intimacy
- **Reconciliation vs closure**: The film resists easy resolution, offering instead a provisional, fragile peace

## Critical Reception
Kapoor and Sons was critically acclaimed for its mature, unsentimental screenplay and its exceptional ensemble performances. Rishi Kapoor's portrayal of the 90-year-old Dadu under heavy prosthetics was particularly praised.
