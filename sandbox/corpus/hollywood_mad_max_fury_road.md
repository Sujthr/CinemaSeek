# Mad Max: Fury Road (2015)

## Overview
Mad Max: Fury Road is a 2015 Australian post-apocalyptic action film directed and co-written by George Miller. The film stars Tom Hardy as Max Rockatansky and Charlize Theron as Imperator Furiosa. It won six Academy Awards including Best Film Editing, Best Production Design, Best Costume Design, Best Makeup, Best Sound Editing, and Best Sound Mixing — more than any other film that year.

## Plot
In a future wasteland dominated by Immortan Joe (Hugh Keays-Byrne), a warlord who controls the water supply and uses it to subjugate a desperate population, Furiosa (Charlize Theron) — one of Joe's lieutenants — smuggles his five enslaved wives ("breeders") to freedom in a massive war rig. Max (Tom Hardy), initially a captive used as a blood bag for one of Joe's War Boys, escapes and reluctantly joins Furiosa's mission.

The film is essentially one continuous high-speed chase across a desert wasteland — pursued by Joe's war party, navigating through dying communities, searching for the mythical "Green Place." When the Green Place proves to be a toxic swamp, Furiosa and Max must turn back and take Joe's citadel itself. The film ends with them returning, triumphant, and opening the water to the enslaved masses below.

## Themes
- **Female agency and ensemble heroism**: Furiosa is the film's true protagonist; the film is fundamentally about women refusing to be property
- **Dystopian class hierarchy**: Immortan Joe's control of water as a metaphor for resource monopoly and political power
- **Road movie liberation**: The film is structured as a road movie — movement as freedom, stasis as death
- **Witness and testimony**: The War Boy Nux's arc from true believer to ally mirrors the film's themes of propaganda and awakening
- **Redemption**: Max's arc is one of reintegration — from solitary trauma survivor to someone who acts for others
