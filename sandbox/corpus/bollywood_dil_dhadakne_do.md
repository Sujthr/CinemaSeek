# Dil Dhadakne Do (2015)

## Overview
Dil Dhadakne Do is a 2015 Indian Hindi-language ensemble family drama directed by Zoya Akhtar and produced by Ritesh Sidhwani and Farhan Akhtar under Excel Entertainment. The film stars Anil Kapoor, Shefali Shah, Ranveer Singh, Priyanka Chopra, and Farhan Akhtar. It was narrated by Pluto, the family dog, voiced by Aamir Khan.

## Plot
The Mehras are a wealthy Delhi family who take a Mediterranean cruise to celebrate their thirtieth wedding anniversary. Patriarch Kamal Mehra (Anil Kapoor) is a businessman whose empire is quietly crumbling. His wife Neelam (Shefali Shah) holds the family together through a facade of appearances and control. Their children are in various stages of quiet desperation: daughter Ayesha (Priyanka Chopra) is in a loveless, transactional marriage that keeps her from pursuing her business dreams, while son Kabir (Ranveer Singh) is being pressured into marrying a suitable girl when he's secretly in love with someone his parents would never approve.

Over the ten days of the cruise, each family member confronts their private truths, and the polished surface of the Mehra family finally cracks. Relationships are re-examined, betrayals are surfaced, and each character is forced to choose between comfort and authenticity.

## Themes
Dil Dhadakne Do is a sharp ensemble study of upper-class Indian family dysfunction:
- **Class and entitlement**: The privilege and insular world-view of India's ultra-wealthy
- **Gender roles**: The double standards applied to Ayesha (expected to subordinate her ambitions to her husband's) versus Kabir (pressured to marry for business advantage)
- **Family as prison**: How familial expectations trap individuals in roles they never chose
- **Performance of success**: The gap between the family's public image and private reality
- **Liberation**: Each character's tentative journey toward authentic selfhood

## Ensemble Structure and Narrative Technique
Zoya Akhtar's great skill is in managing multiple character arcs simultaneously without losing narrative coherence. Each family member — including the extended cast of old friends aboard the ship — gets a meaningful arc with genuine emotional stakes. The cruise ship setting functions as a pressure cooker: a finite space, finite time, no escape from each other or from long-suppressed truths.

## Directorial Style
Zoya Akhtar's direction is elegant and observational. She allows long scenes to breathe, trusting her ensemble cast to carry emotional weight through performance rather than melodrama. The film is visually lush — gorgeous Mediterranean locations — but uses this beauty ironically, setting private anguish against spectacular backdrop.

## Critical Reception
The film received widespread critical acclaim for its performances, screenplay, and Akhtar's assured direction. Priyanka Chopra and Ranveer Singh were particularly praised, with Singh winning multiple awards for his performance.
