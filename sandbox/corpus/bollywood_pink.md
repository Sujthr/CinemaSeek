# Pink (2016)

## Overview
Pink is a 2016 Indian Hindi-language legal thriller film directed by Aniruddha Roy Chowdhury and produced by Shoojit Sircar and Rashmi Sharma. The film stars Amitabh Bachchan as Deepak Sehgal, a retired lawyer who takes up the defence of three young women — Minal (Taapsee Pannu), Falak (Kirti Kulhari), and Andrea (Andrea Tariang) — who are accused of assaulting a young man after they reject his sexual advances.

## Plot
Three young working women in Delhi attend a party with a group of men. When one of the women rejects a man's advances and he becomes aggressive, she injures him in self-defence. The man, from a politically connected family, files assault charges against them. The women face not just legal jeopardy but systematic character assassination: their social lives, clothing choices, and independence are used as evidence of "loose character."

The retired lawyer Deepak Sehgal (Amitabh Bachchan), who lives next door and is battling his own mental health crisis, takes their case. In the climactic courtroom sequences, Sehgal argues the film's central thesis through meticulous cross-examination: "no means no" — regardless of a woman's clothes, her past, her relationship with the accused, or the circumstances of their meeting. The film builds to a verdict that is less a legal triumph than a moral statement.

## Themes
- **Consent**: The film's primary argument — that a woman's "no" is absolute and non-negotiable
- **Victim blaming and slut-shaming**: How women who report sexual violence are subjected to character assassination
- **Gender and class**: Powerful men's assumption that money and connections insulate them from consequences
- **Independence and moral judgment**: Working women's autonomy misread as moral permissiveness
- **Legal system as patriarchy**: How courts can replicate and reinforce the structures that harm women

## Critical Reception
Pink was one of the most important Indian films of 2016. Critics and audiences responded to its direct engagement with consent and sexual violence. Amitabh Bachchan's courtroom performance was considered one of his finest.
