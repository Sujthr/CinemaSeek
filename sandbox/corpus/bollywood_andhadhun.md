# Andhadhun (2018)

## Overview
Andhadhun is a 2018 Indian Hindi-language black comedy thriller film directed by Sriram Raghavan. The film stars Ayushmann Khurrana as Akash, a blind pianist; Tabu as Simi, a femme fatale; and Radhika Apte as Sophie. It received universal critical acclaim and won multiple National Film Awards, including Best Hindi Film and Best Adapted Screenplay. Ayushmann Khurrana won the National Film Award for Best Actor.

## Plot
Akash, a pianist who pretends to be blind to enhance his musical sensitivity, witnesses the murder of retired film actor Pramod Sinha — or does he? His blindness (fake and real) becomes a labyrinth of moral ambiguity. Simi, Pramod's much-younger wife, was the killer, and once she suspects Akash can see, he becomes a target. What follows is a tightly wound neo-noir thriller where deception layers upon deception: Akash's pretend blindness, Simi's guileless performance for investigators, and a series of escalating complications involving a doctor who harvests organs, a cricket bat, and a missing cat.

The film plays with the question of sight and knowledge throughout — what does Akash actually see? What does he know? And what choice does he make at the crucial moment?

## Themes
- **Moral ambiguity**: The film delights in leaving its ethical questions unresolved
- **Deception and performance**: Nearly every character is performing a role for someone else
- **Neo-noir femme fatale**: Simi is a brilliant, amoral antagonist in the classic noir tradition
- **Karma and complicity**: Akash's choices imply he is not entirely innocent
- **Dark comedy**: The film mines extreme situations for uncomfortable, brilliant humour

## Critical Reception
Andhadhun was a sleeper hit that became one of the most celebrated Hindi films of 2018. Critics praised Sriram Raghavan's direction, Tabu's electrifying performance, and the screenplay's ingenious plotting. The film was India's official entry for the Academy Awards and became one of the highest-grossing Bollywood films in China.
