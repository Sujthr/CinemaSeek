# Bard of Blood (2019)

## Overview
Bard of Blood is a 2019 Indian Hindi-language spy thriller web series adapted as a film, directed by Ribhu Dasgupta and produced by Netflix India and Red Chillies Entertainment. Based on Bilal Siddiqi's novel of the same name, the film stars Emraan Hashmi as Kabir Anand, a disgraced RAW agent recalled for a covert mission in Balochistan, Pakistan. The title references Shakespeare's "Macbeth."

## Plot
Kabir Anand (Emraan Hashmi) is a former Research and Analysis Wing (RAW) operative who was dishonourably discharged after a mission in Afghanistan went wrong. He now teaches Shakespeare at a college in Patan, Gujarat. When four Indian intelligence agents are captured in Balochistan and face execution, the deputy director of RAW (Jaideep Ahlawat) secretly activates Kabir for a deniable extraction mission.

Accompanied by analyst Isha Khanna (Sobhita Dhulipala) and a Pashto-speaking agent Veer Singh (Viineet Kumar), Kabir enters the tribal badlands of Balochistan. The mission is complicated by double agents, tribal alliances, Pakistani military intelligence (ISI), and Kabir's own traumatic past. The film builds toward a race-against-time extraction climax.

## Themes
- **Espionage and deniability**: The film explores the moral ambiguity of state-sponsored covert operations
- **Redemption and second chances**: Kabir's mission as an attempt to redeem a career defined by a single catastrophic failure
- **Shakespeare as subtext**: The series uses Macbeth — ambition, betrayal, conscience — as a thematic mirror
- **Geopolitical complexity**: The Kashmir-Balochistan axis and the intelligence war between India and Pakistan
- **Identity under pressure**: Characters whose professional identities have been stripped and must be rebuilt
