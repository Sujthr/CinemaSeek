# Taare Zameen Par (2007)

## Overview
Taare Zameen Par (Every Child is Special) is a 2007 Indian Hindi-language drama film directed by Aamir Khan in his directorial debut. The film stars Darsheel Safary as Ishaan Awasthi, a dyslexic eight-year-old boy, and Aamir Khan as Ram Shankar Nikumbh, an art teacher who recognises Ishaan's hidden brilliance. It received critical and commercial acclaim, winning four Filmfare Awards and the National Film Award for Best Direction.

## Plot
Ishaan Awasthi is a creative, spirited child who struggles to read, write, and follow instructions. Misunderstood by his teachers and frustrated parents, he is labelled as lazy and troublesome. When his parents send him to a boarding school, Ishaan withdraws further into isolation and depression. The arrival of the unconventional art teacher Nikumbh changes everything. He identifies Ishaan's dyslexia, advocates for him to the school administration, and uses patient, creative teaching methods to help Ishaan unlock his potential. The film culminates in a school art competition where Ishaan's extraordinary talent is finally recognised.

## Themes
- **Neurodiversity and education**: A tender portrait of how schools can fail children who learn differently
- **Parental pressure**: The damage done when parents prioritise achievement over understanding
- **Childhood and wonder**: The film is suffused with the sensory richness of Ishaan's imaginative inner world
- **The transformative teacher**: How a single empathetic adult can change a child's trajectory
- **Artistic expression as intelligence**: The film argues that creativity is as valid as academic performance

## Critical Reception
The film was praised for its sensitive handling of dyslexia and its performances, particularly Darsheel Safary's naturalistic portrayal of Ishaan. International critics noted its emotional depth and its sharp critique of competitive Indian education.
