# Queen (2014)

## Overview
Queen is a 2014 Indian Hindi-language coming-of-age road film directed by Vikas Bahl and produced by Anurag Kashyap, Vikramaditya Motwane, and Madhu Mantena. The film stars Kangana Ranaut as Rani, a sheltered Delhi girl who decides to take her honeymoon to Paris and Amsterdam alone after her fiancé calls off the wedding at the last minute. It won the National Film Award for Best Hindi Film and Kangana Ranaut won the Filmfare Award for Best Actress.

## Plot
Rani Mehra is a traditional, sheltered girl from Rajouri Garden, Delhi. On the eve of her wedding, her NRI fiancé Vijay (Rajkummar Rao) breaks off the engagement, citing that she is too "small-town" for his new cosmopolitan life. Rather than surrender to shame and stay home, Rani decides to take the honeymoon trip to Paris and Amsterdam alone.

In Paris, she stumbles through the city, bewildered and alone, slowly finding her footing. She befriends Vijayalakshmi (Lisa Haydon), a free-spirited French-Indian woman who introduces her to a bigger world. In Amsterdam, she stays in a hostel with three male roommates from different countries and learns to befriend men as equals without romantic expectation. By the time Vijay comes back seeking reconciliation, Rani has discovered a self she never knew existed — and she makes a quiet, powerful choice.

## Themes
- **Female self-discovery**: The transformative journey from dependency to autonomy
- **Road movie liberation**: Travel as a mechanism for personal reinvention
- **Class and aspiration**: The gap between Rani's traditional background and the global modernity she encounters
- **Male gaze and female identity**: Rani's gradual unlearning of the need for male approval
- **Quiet rebellion**: The film's feminist message is delivered gently but unmistakably

## Critical Reception
Queen is considered one of the finest feminist films in Indian cinema. Kangana Ranaut's performance — moving from timidity to quiet confidence — was universally praised. The film's depiction of a solo female traveller finding herself resonated widely.
